02 / The bet

A recurring workflow with measurable potential

Technical asset managers spend time interpreting operational events, assigning categories and documenting the reasons.

Current workflow

Read event data and service reports → interpret cause → categorize → comment → review.

Proposed workflow

Rules first → AI-assisted classification with evidence → expert review for exceptions and critical cases.
The hypothesis: at least 70% of all in-scope events can be correctly automated and manual processing effort reduced by at least 50%.

Proposed pilot gates, not measured outcomes. Compare the native platform option before committing to development.

07 / The guardrails

Govern the workflow before deployment

Allowed

Authorized data retrieval, analysis and drafting. Validated low-risk write-back only after approval gates.

Not allowed

Autonomous OT control, financial commitments, unapproved data transfers or autonomous agents in v1.

Compounding

Capture expert corrections; prove improvement on separate holdout data. No cross-customer reuse without rights.

Governance

Assign owners, review security and data rights, document AI risk classification, monitor quality and cost continuously.

15/25 provisional maturity self-assessment; 10 shadow-AI hypotheses, zero verified. Regulatory assessment and formal approval are pending.
